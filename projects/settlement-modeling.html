THE PROJECT 01 / 03

Overview

Research Assistant work in the University at Buffalo Department of Geography, applying supervised learning to settlement category prediction in Lower Fungom, Cameroon.

KEY QUESTIONS 02 / 03

Questions guiding the work

  1. Which environmental characteristics help predict settlement categories?
  2. How well do different models generalize?
  3. What limitations arise when inferring settlement patterns from geographic predictors?
METHODS & NEXT STEPS 03 / 03

Methods and materials

Random Forest, XGBoost, and seven environmental and topographic variables, including elevation, slope, curvature, and visibility.
