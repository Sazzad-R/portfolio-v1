Air Pollution and Environmental Inequality in New York.

An ongoing spatial examination of PM2.5 exposure in relation to income and racial composition.

THE PROJECT 01 / 03

Overview

An evolving spatial statistical project investigating whether patterns of modeled PM2.5 exposure vary with census-tract socioeconomic and demographic characteristics in New York State.

KEY QUESTIONS 02 / 03

Questions guiding the work

  1. How does PM2.5 exposure vary geographically?
  2. What associations appear with income and racial composition?
  3. How robust are observed associations to spatial dependence and modeling choices?
METHODS & NEXT STEPS 03 / 03

Methods and materials

Census-tract data, ACS covariates, PM2.5 exposure estimates, mapping, spatial statistics and sensitivity analysis.

Before publishing this project

Work in progress. No final findings, causal claims, or validated maps are presented in this draft.
